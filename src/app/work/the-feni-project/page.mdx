## The Brief

Feni House needed a complete visual identity overhaul — from packaging to digital presence — that honored the traditional Goan spirit while appealing to modern consumers.

## The Constraint

Tight timeline of 2 weeks and a limited budget meant every pixel had to count. No room for scope creep, no endless revision cycles.

## What I'd Change Now

Looking back, I would have pushed harder for a dedicated photoshoot rather than relying on stock-adjacent assets. The concept was strong, but the final execution would have hit harder with custom photography.
